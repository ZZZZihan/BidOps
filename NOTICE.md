# Source and submission policy

No government procurement authority, competition organizer or vendor is affiliated with this repo. No real business has agreed to pilot or endorsed the product. Public announcements contain official data that may change. BidOps is **decision support with human verification**, not legal/procurement advice or a bid submission system.

Do not add participant phone/email, private vendor profiles, portal accounts, fetched raw documents, or third-party images/files to GitHub. Refer to original official notice links.
